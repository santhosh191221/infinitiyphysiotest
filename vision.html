About Us

Care with purpose.
Rehabilitation with meaning.

Our Story

Helping people move forward with confidence.

Infinity Physio & Rehab was founded by two passionate physiotherapists, Dr. Keerthana, PT, MIAP, and Dr. Tamilvasan, PT, with a shared vision: to make quality physiotherapy accessible, meaningful, and focused on each individual's needs.

We believe physiotherapy is more than treating pain. It is about restoring movement, rebuilding confidence, improving independence, and helping people return to everyday life with greater ease.

Through evidence-based practice, personalised treatment plans, and compassionate care, we aim to make every step of rehabilitation purposeful.

Our Founders

Led by purpose. Grounded in care.

Dr. Keerthana, PT, MIAP

Founder · Registration No. 69211

A graduate of Meenakshi College of Physiotherapy, Dr. Keerthana completed internships at MAHER, Gericare, and Tamil Nadu Government Multi-Super-Speciality Hospital (Omanthurar). Her clinical experience spans orthopaedics, geriatrics, neurology, oncology, and sports rehabilitation.

Currently pursuing her Master's in Orthopaedics, she is passionate about musculoskeletal and occupational health, with research focused on musculoskeletal disorders among weavers and factory workers.

Dr. Tamilvasan, PT

Co-Founder · CEO · Chief Consultant Physiotherapist

Dr. Tamilvasan trained at MAHER, NIPMED, and Tamil Nadu Government Multi-Super-Speciality Hospital (Omanthurar), developing expertise in paediatrics, neurology, oncology, sports, and cardiology rehabilitation.

Inspired by Dr. T. Bhuvaneshwaran, PT, Chief Consultant Physiotherapist, a great mentor and the reason he chose this field, Dr. Tamilvasan's philosophy is rooted in guidance and a commitment to excellence. His research on fear of falling in older adults reflects his specialised interest in geriatric physiotherapy. With leadership and dedication, he drives the vision of Infinity Physio and Rehab as a trusted rehabilitation brand.
